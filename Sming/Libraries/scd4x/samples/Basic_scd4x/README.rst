SCD4x CO2 sensor
================

SCD4x is Sensirion's compact family of photoacoustic NDIR CO₂ sensors for indoor-air-quality monitoring.
- Measures CO₂, temperature, and relative humidity.
- Uses a digital I²C interface and comes in a small 10.1 x 10.1 x 6.5 mm package.
- SCD40: cost-effective, standard accuracy, specified from 400-2,000 ppm.
- SCD41: improved accuracy and 400-5,000 ppm range, with low-power single-shot mode.
- SCD43: highest accuracy, 400-5,000 ppm range, and broader building-standard compatibility.
They are commonly used in ventilation controls, air-quality monitors, smart-home devices, and building-management systems
